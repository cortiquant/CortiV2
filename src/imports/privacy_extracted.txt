This Privacy Policy explains what personal and health data Cortiquant collects, why we 
collect it, how we use and protect it, and your rights over your data. Please read this 
carefully before using the Cortiquant platform. 
 
1. WHO WE ARE 
 
Cortiquant ("we", "us", "our") is a healthtech company developing an AI-powered workplace 
stress analytics platform.For privacy-related queries, contact us at: cortiqsupport@gmail.com  
 
 
2. WHAT DATA WE COLLECT 
 
2.1 Personal Identification Data 
• Employment information — organisation name, job title, department, and work location 
• Account credentials — username and encrypted password 
 
2.2 Health & Biological Data 
This is the most sensitive category of data we collect. We handle it with the highest level of 
care, encryption, and access controls. 
• Mind-Body Stress Index scores — AI-generated composite stress scores derived from 
biomarker and psychometric inputs 
• Burnout risk ratings — individual and longitudinal burnout risk classifications generated 
by the platform 
 
2.3 Psychometric & Self-Report Data 
• Perceived Stress Scale (PSS-10) responses and total scores 
• Mood and affect assessment responses

2.4 Usage & Technical Data 
• Device type, operating system, browser type, and IP address 
• Login timestamps, session duration, and in-app activity logs 
• Crash reports and error logs (anonymized) for platform improvement 
 
 
3. WHY WE COLLECT YOUR DATA — PURPOSES OF PROCESSING 
 
We collect and process your data only for the following specific, legitimate purposes: 
 
1. To generate your personalised Mind-Body Stress Index and provide you with your 
stress score, trend analysis, and burnout risk indicators through the platform dashboard. 
2. To enable your employer's HR team to view anonymised, aggregated workforce stress 
trends — individual data is never shared with your employer without your explicit 
consent. 
3. To train and improve Cortiquant's AI stress prediction model using anonymised, 
aggregated data — we will never use your identifiable data for model training without 
your prior written consent. 
4. To communicate with you about your account, results, platform updates, and wellness 
resources. 
5. To comply with applicable laws, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection 
(DPDP) Act, 2023. 
 
 
4. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING 
 
We process your data on the following legal grounds under India's DPDP Act, 2023 and 
applicable regulations: 
• Explicit Informed Consent — you provide consent at account registration and before 
each biomarker test or psychometric assessment. Consent is specific, informed, and 
freely given. 
• Contractual Necessity — processing necessary to provide you the services you have 
agreed to use. 
• Legitimate Interest — anonymised, aggregated data used for product improvement and 
research, where your fundamental rights are not overridden. 
• Legal Compliance — processing required to comply with applicable Indian laws and 
regulations.

5. HOW WE SHARE YOUR DATA 
 
Cortiquant does not sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data to any third party. Ever. 
 
5.1 With Your Employer 
Your employer's HR dashboard shows only anonymised, aggregated workforce-level data (e.g., 
average stress index by department). Your individual score, biomarker values, and psychometric 
responses are NEVER shared with your employer without your explicit, specific, written consent. 
 
5.2 With Laboratory Partners 
If biomarker blood testing is facilitated through the platform, your blood sample and minimum 
necessary information (anonymised participant ID, test type) is shared with our certified 
laboratory partners solely for the purpose of conducting the agreed tests. 
 
5.3 With Technology Service Providers 
We use AWS (Amazon Web Services) for cloud hosting and data storage. AWS processes data 
on our behalf under a Data Processing Agreement and does not have independent access to 
your identifiable data. 
 
5.4 Legal Disclosure 
We may disclose your data if required by a court order, applicable law, or government authority. 
We will notify you of any such requirement unless legally prohibited from doing so. 
 
 
6. HEALTH DATA — SPECIAL PROTECTIONS 
 
Biological and psychological health data is classified as Sensitive Personal Data under Indian 
law and receives the following additional protections on the Cortiquant platform: 
• Encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+) for all health data stored on AWS 
infrastructure 
• Role-based access control — only you and explicitly authorised Cortiquant personnel 
with a legitimate need can access your identifiable health data 
• Health data is stored in a separate, access-controlled database tier segregated from 
general platform data 
• No health data is used for advertising, marketing profiling, or sold to any third party

7. DATA RETENTION 
 
We retain your data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy: 
• Account and profile data: Retained for the duration of your active account  
• Individual health data (biomarker results, PSS scores, Stress Index): Retained till the 
time you have active account 
• Anonymised aggregated data: May be retained indefinitely for research and product 
improvement 
• Audit and activity logs: Retained for 2 months for security and compliance purposes 
Upon expiry of retention periods or upon your valid deletion request, data is permanently deleted 
from all production and backup systems within 30 days. 
 
 
8. YOUR RIGHTS UNDER INDIA'S DPDP ACT, 2023 
 
As a Data Principal under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the 
following rights: 
• Right to Access — you may request a summary of what personal data we hold about 
you and how it is being processed 
• Right to Correction — you may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal 
data 
• Right to Erasure — you may request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal 
retention obligations 
• Right to Withdraw Consent — you may withdraw your consent to data processing at any 
time; this will not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal but will require 
account closure 
• Right to Grievance Redressal — you may raise a complaint with our Data Protection 
Officer (DPO) and, if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India 
 
To exercise any of these rights, email us at: cortiqsupport@gmail.com . We will respond within 
30 days.
