What this form is about 
You are about to sign up as a participant in Cortiquant's pilot testing program. As part of this program, the Cortiquant app will collect 
stress-related data through your responses and (where applicable) diagnostic inputs. A portion of this data will be used for research and 
scientific validation of our platform. 
  
WHAT DATA IS COLLECTED 
• Stress scores and responses from the Mind Stress Index (MSI) questionnaire 
• Stress category classification based on MSI composite score 
• Usage patterns within the Cortiquant application 
  
HOW IT IS USED 
• Research validation and scientific accuracy testing of the 
Cortiquant platform 
• Improving algorithm performance and stress classification 
models 
• Internal product development — not for commercial sale or 
third-party sharing 
YOUR PRIVACY PROTECTIONS 
• All data is de-identified — your name, contact, and employer 
details are removed before any research use 
• No personally identifiable information (PII) will appear in any 
research output or report 
• Data is stored securely and accessible only to the Cortiquant 
research team 
  
Your Rights as a Participant 
You may withdraw your participation at any time. Withdrawal will not affect your employment or standing with your organization. To 
withdraw or request deletion of your data, contact us at: contact@cortiquant.com
